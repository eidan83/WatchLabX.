WatchLabX Privacy Policy

Publisher: LabX
Application: WatchLabX for Android
Effective date: October 14, 2026

WatchLabX is an offline tool for analyzing the timing of mechanical watches. This policy explains how the app handles information on your device.

Microphone access

WatchLabX requests microphone permission to capture sound signals from a mechanical watch and calculate timing measurements. Audio is processed locally on your device for this purpose. The app does not upload microphone audio to a LabX server.

Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, WatchLabX may store watch profiles, watch photos, timing measurements, test history, settings, and local diagnostic logs on your device. This information is used to support measurements, reports, and app operation. The app does not require an account to perform watch analysis.

Network services and third parties

Core watch analysis does not require an internet connection. WatchLabX does not transmit watch measurements or microphone recordings to LabX servers. An optional action may open a Google search in your device's browser; information you choose to submit there is governed by that external service's privacy practices.

Device backups

Depending on your Android version and device backup settings, locally stored app data may be included in Android's operating-system backup or restore services. These services are controlled by your device and account settings, not by a WatchLabX-operated server.

Sharing and exports

If you choose to export or share a report or other information, the destination and its handling of the information are determined by your choice and the receiving app or service.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored watch information remains on your device until you delete it through available app controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to any copies retained by device backup services or exports you created.

Children

WatchLabX is a technical utility for mechanical-watch analysis and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if WatchLabX's features or data-handling practices change. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information provided on the WatchLabX Google Play store listing.
